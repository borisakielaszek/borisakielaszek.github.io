Boris Akielaszek is an expressionist and symbolist painter, author, illustrator and board game designer. For more than twenty years, he has explored the boundaries between reality and imagination, symbolism, strangeness and humour. Based near Forbach in Moselle, he paints and writes in his studio in Oeting.

His work spans painting, fantasy, dystopia, heroic fantasy and illustrated books. He also writes under pseudonyms whose connection to his public identity he keeps private. His Vodou painting series Zétwal will be the subject of an illustrated book planned for the first quarter of 2027.

With his daughter Stella Akielaszek-Seewald, he creates the COCASSE collection: six illustrated albums and a bonus book, combining humour, strangeness and different levels of reading with the imagination of Moselle and the Franco-German border region. He also develops The Vampires of Saarbrücken as a book and a card game.

Painting & series

From crowned figures to the spirits of Vodou: painting shaped by emotion and symbol.

Poétique de la Reine

Poetics of the Queen

Developed over several years and still ongoing, Poétique de la Reine explores womanhood, love and feminine presence through a recurring crowned figure. From one canvas to the next, this archetypal figure embodies the search for an immeasurable and unconditional love.

Catharsis & Sublimation

Tensions and emotions take shape in colours, figures and symbols. The series explores what painting can shift and transform within the inner world.

Le Réel projeté

Projected Reality

Observing the world, feeling it, then reimagining it on canvas: the series brings what is seen into dialogue with the artist’s inner experience.

Illustrated tales

COCASSE collection — with Stella

The COCASSE collection grew out of a father-daughter collaboration between Boris Akielaszek and Stella Akielaszek-Seewald. Its illustrated children’s books combine humour, absurdity, strangeness and different levels of reading, with a strong connection to Moselle and the Franco-German border region.

A little ghost hides in the illustrations: a game of observation that extends the pleasure of reading.

Alongside the six albums, The Secret Life of Toys: A Photographic Toy Adventure is a COCASSE bonus book. Several titles in the collection have been translated into many languages.

Games

Two vampire clans, one night in Saarbrücken and dirty tricks to keep their blood.

The Vampires of Saarbrücken — The card game

The Vampires of Saarbrücken features seven vampires: members of the von Dunkelwald and Del Rattello clans, together with their illustrious ancestor Draco von Blutstern.

During a long night in Saarbrücken, fragile alliances, attacks, thefts and dirty tricks follow one another. Each player normally starts with 5 drops of blood and must protect them while the others try to bleed them dry.

The goal is simple: survive the night.

The game combines characters, attacks, defences, events and places inspired by Saarbrücken and the Franco-German border region.

2–5 players · Ages 10+ · Around 15 minutes per game

The English edition is now available to buy on DriveThruCards. French and German editions are planned for 2027.
