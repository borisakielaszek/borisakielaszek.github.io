Books

Novels & stories

Boris Akielaszek has published fantastic, dystopian and heroic-fantasy novels. His writing explores the friction between reality and imagination, memory, inner tensions, archetypes and the darker side of his characters.

Monograph & painting

His work as an author also includes a monograph devoted to painting, extending in words a long-standing exploration of image, material and symbolism.

Les Vampires de Sarrebruck

A Franco-German children's universe combining humour, fantasy, satire and real places in Saarbrücken and Forbach.

COCASSE Collection — with Stella

The COCASSE collection grew out of a father-daughter collaboration between Boris Akielaszek and his daughter Stella Akielaszek-Seewald. Six books have already been published in the collection. Together they create illustrated children's books where humour, absurdity, strangeness and layered reading meet. With each new volume, the collection is becoming increasingly rooted in their local area, bringing into its stories places, legends and an imaginary world connected with Moselle and the Franco-German border region. Several titles have been translated and published in many languages, including English, German, Spanish, Italian, Polish, Dutch, Swedish, Norwegian, Danish and Finnish.

Zétwal — work in progress

Building on the Zétwal painting series, Boris Akielaszek is currently working on an illustrated book devoted to Vodou. The book places his paintings in dialogue with research into the figures, symbols and legacies that run through this world.

About

Boris Akielaszek is an expressionist and symbolist painter, author, illustrator and board game designer. For more than twenty years, he has explored the points of friction between reality and imagination, memory, symbolism, strangeness and humour. His work unfolds through painting, novels, illustrated books and game creation.

In literature, he has written across several genres — fantastic fiction, dystopia and heroic fantasy — while developing projects in which text and image answer one another. He is also the author of a monograph on painting. With Zétwal, a painting series devoted to Vodou, he is now developing an illustrated book built around his own works and the figures, symbols and legacies they explore.

With his daughter Stella Akielaszek-Seewald, he also forms the author duo behind the COCASSE collection, a series of illustrated children's books in which absurdity, humour, strangeness and sensitivity meet. Six books have already been published, and the collection is becoming increasingly rooted in the local culture and imagination of Moselle and the Franco-German border region. He is also the creator of the Les Vampires de Sarrebruck universe, developed as both a book and a card game. Based in Oeting near Forbach, Moselle, he develops part of his work within the Franco-German cultural space.
